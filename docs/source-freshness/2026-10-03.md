# ODOP official-source freshness review

Checked: 2026-10-03

Freshness status: `checked_not_assumed_current`

## Result

The live MoFPI ODOP landing page still identifies only `REVISED LIST OF ODOP FOR 35 STATES
13.03.2024` as its downloadable list. Newer official publications exist, but none is an explicit
revision, replacement, or superseding approval. They also conflict with one another and with the
2024 approval memorandum at the district/product level.

The 2024 revised list therefore remains the v0 source-of-record because it is the currently linked,
explicitly approved revision—not because it is assumed to be the latest possible data. The newer
documents are retained as reconciliation sources.

## Supersession assessment

The 2024 document is an approval memorandum that explicitly ratifies seven revised Andhra Pradesh
assignments and approves 13 newly formed districts. The newer annual report's annexure reverts several
of those revised assignments—for example, it shows East Godavari as Coconut and Krishna as Mango,
while the approval memorandum changes them to Banana and Fish & Shrimp based products. That annexure
cannot safely be treated as a superseding list without clarification.

The February 2025 parliamentary answer is newer and describes its 726-row annexure as approved as of
2025-01-31. It contains wording changes and some assignment differences, but it does not identify
itself as a revision or replacement. It is important reconciliation evidence, not an automatic
supersession signal.

The annual report states 726 districts and 137 unique products, so neither the official row count nor
the official product-family count changed. The parliamentary answer confirms the 726-row count but
does not state a unique-product count.

## Operational guard

The machine-readable record is [`config/source_freshness.toml`](../../config/source_freshness.toml).
Canonical ingestion validates the configured SHA-256 and 726-row invariant and fails closed if either
changes. A changed source requires a new freshness review and an explicit supersession decision.
